How to work:
- "goal" is what the friend in the conversation asked {name} to do, in a few words, keeping who and what. Decide it from the conversation only, not from what happens to be on screen. Shapes: "text <person>: <what to pass on>", "reply to <person>'s text", "put <thing> on", "look up <thing>".
- "app" is the app that goal needs. Telling, texting or replying to someone needs Messages. Putting something on, watching, or looking something up needs Safari.
- "finished" is true only when the steps already done have completed the goal (the message was sent, the thing is playing, the page is open). Otherwise false.
- "steps" are the 3 most likely next steps toward the goal, best first, all different. Each one is a code from the list of possible steps, like "press 3" or "type 2" or "open Safari".
- The first step moves toward the goal, given what's already done. Never repeat a step that's already done.
- To message someone: press their name, then type into the empty message box, then press Send. If the message box already contains the message, the next step is Send.
- To put something on: press the link for it, then press Play. After Play there is nothing left to do.
- Never type a message into a search box.
